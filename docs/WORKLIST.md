# The worklist

One ordered list, worked strictly in order, one item at a time (Law 10). Take the first open item.
A new item is **inserted** at the position its dependencies put it, never appended; the record says
where it landed and why. An item is done when its checks are green, `docs/RECORD.md` has its entry,
and `docs/COVERAGE.md` is re-marked, all in one commit (Law 14).

> **BUILD FORWARD.** A session's work is the next item on this list. Every bug found on the way goes
> in `docs/IMPLEMENTATION.md` — every one, with what was measured and where — and the item carries on. Tests
> are written as the item goes and RUN at the end of a full module, never during one: a suite run
> mid-item measures a half-built world and reports the half that is missing (Law 11). The one thing
> fixed on the spot is a violation that stops the build — an impossible quantity, a one-sided flow,
> a fact with two writers — because the engine will not run past it. See `docs/PLAN.md` §5.

**THIS LIST IS HISTORY, AND `docs/IMPLEMENTATION.md` IS THE PLAN.** Every row here that was open is
carried by a plan item and says which; nothing is worked from this file any more. It stays because
it is the ledger of what was worked and closed (Law 13), and it is the one writer of that: a `done`
row is what tells `plan:progress` to count a closed item's steps, whose section was deleted when it
closed. Item 13 was split into 13a–13o as the plan was written; the split is stated in each entry.

**The ids collided and that is why the rows moved.** This list's `14` was the polity and the plan's
`14` is the insurers; `15`, `16` and `17` differed the same way. Two files using one id for two
items is the thing item 0c exists to end, so the plan's ids win — it is the ordered list — and each
superseded row here points at the item that carries it.

> **WHAT `done` MEANS, MEASURED.** `docs/IMPLEMENTATION.md` read every `done` row below and every
> `MET` in `docs/COVERAGE.md` against the code, and eight of these rows are for mechanisms that are
> assembled, compile, and have **never produced an outcome**: **13f** (no corporate bond is ever
> issued, and nothing ever borrows a security), **13h** (no insurance party is ever created),
> **13g** (a takeover buys the shares and never combines the company), **13d** (no order has ever
> reached the tenancy venue, and nobody can buy a dwelling), **9** (no bank quotes in any market),
> **13a/13b** (the derivative layer and its nine classes have never produced a contract) and
> **13e** (the securitisation waterfall never allocates a loss). The rows stay `done` because the
> items were worked and closed and this list is not rewritten backwards (Law 13); the findings are
> B-1 to B-8 and none has been positioned into an item yet. A row saying `done` says the item was
> worked, not that its mechanism has ever run.
