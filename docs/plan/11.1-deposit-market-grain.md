# Item 11.1 — A class of depositors that does not move as one block

**Objective.** A rate difference one basis point past what moving costs a class moves EVERY member of
that class in the same week, because every depositor of a class faces the same two numbers and
answers them the same way. That makes a bank's whole funding base a step function, and it makes the
cell grain load-bearing on a macro outcome — which XI-15 says is a finding about the resolution and
Law 2 says is a defect in it. Until it is fixed the last placeholder in the engine cannot be
deleted: item 11 tried twice and both replacements re-rolled which whole cells crossed between banks.

**Read first.** §24 Banks Funding A1, A1.a–d, B1.a, E1, E2, E4; XI-15 (all, especially the standing
resolution measurement); §46 XI-16; `docs/RECORD.md` **11**, findings 1 and 7. Code:
`money-market/deposits.ts` (`moveDeposits`, `setRates`, `announced`), `money-market/index.ts`
(`worthOfMoney`), `sovereign-curve/index.ts` (`bufferTarget`, `P_BUFFER`), `test/resolution/cells.test.ts`.

**Clauses this item meets.** Banks Funding A1.d, B1.a (fully), E1, E2; Sovereign E2.a, E5; Money
Market A2.a. It deletes `bank.liquidityBuffer.perDeposit`, the last placeholder (XI-14).

## Design

The two rungs item 11 built are the first half: a bank bids up only when paying up on its whole base
is the cheaper answer (B2.b), and a board answers a board rather than sitting still (B1.a). What is
still one block is the DEPOSITOR side: `moveDeposits` asks one question of a class and every member
of it answers identically.

What differs between real depositors of one class is what it costs THEM to move — attention, an
account they cannot be bothered to close, a payroll that lands where it lands. That is a per-party
number and this world has a place for one: XI-16 gives every deciding party its own outlook formed
from its own history, and §46 A3 says the disagreement is load-bearing. The switching cost should be
a draw per depositor around the class's declared level, or the decision should read the depositor's
own outlook of what its bank pays — one of the two, not both, and whichever it is must be a
PREFERENCE with a stated dispersion (§46 B1.a's shape) rather than a coefficient.

With the block broken, the paper target can be derived from what the bank publishes about its own
liabilities (`couldLeave` or the deposit base) without the grain deciding anything, and item 3's
placeholder dies.

## Exit criteria

A rate difference moves part of a class, not all of it; the aggregates at three grains differ by no
more than what whole people do; no placeholder stands anywhere in the engine.

## Guard

XI-15's standing measurement (if the aggregates move, the finding is the resolution); Law 2 (a
resolution is tested by invariance); Law 6 (nothing here is a smoothing coefficient).
