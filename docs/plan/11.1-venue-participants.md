# Item 11.1 — A venue asks parties for schedules the way a market does

**Objective.** A market (`MarketDecl`) gathers its orders from the participants modules declare per
party kind, each evaluated with that party's own `ParticipantView` (`world.ts` `runOne`). A venue
(`VenueDecl` — the labour market, the money-market session books, the raise) has no such door: the
module that clears it builds every party's schedule inside its own phase, out of whatever that
module can reach. So the money market decides what a bank lends and borrows, and the labour market
decides what a firm hires and a household offers, and both decide it with a `MechanismContext` that
sees every party's private state (Observer A4). This item gives a venue the same door a market has,
so a party's schedule into a venue is that party's own module's decision, taken with that party's
own view — which is what 11.2 needs before a bank's treasury can post its own session schedule.

**Read first.** `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` §4.9b (kernel and modules, the participant contract);
Clearing A2, B2, C5; Observer A4; Law 4, Law 15. Code: `world/module.ts` (`ParticipantDecl`,
`SystemModule.participants`), `world/world.ts` (`runOne`, `participantDecls`, `post`/`posted`),
`world/context.ts` (`openVenue`, `post`, `posted`), `clearing/venue.ts`, and the two venue owners
today: `mechanisms/money-market/index.ts` (`runSession`, `parkTheRest`), `mechanisms/labour/index.ts`.

**Clauses this item meets.** Clearing B2 (a participant brings its own schedule — to a venue as to a
market), Observer A4 (a decision about a party is taken with that party's view and no other), Law 4
(one writer of a party's schedule: its own module).

---

## Design

- `world/module.ts`: a `VenueParticipantDecl { partyKind; orders(view: ParticipantView, venue:
  VenueDecl): readonly Order[] }` and `SystemModule.venueParticipants: readonly
  VenueParticipantDecl[]` (default empty in every existing module — assembly treats a missing field
  as none, exactly as `resolves` is optional).
- `world/context.ts`: `MechanismContext.gather(venue: VenueId): void` — the module that clears a
  venue calls it at the top of its clearing phase; the kernel evaluates every declared venue
  participant of every alive party of that kind with that party's own view and `post`s what comes
  back. `post`/`posted` are unchanged, so a clearing module may still add its OWN side (the window's
  offer, the floor's bid: those are the central bank's schedule and the corridor module is its
  module) and a test module may still post by hand.
- `world/world.ts`: `venueParticipantDecls` collected at assembly beside `participantDecls`; `gather`
  mirrors `runOne`'s loop (a ceased party takes no part, Money E4) and posts rather than clearing.
- Nothing moves in this item. The money market and labour keep building schedules inside their
  phases; `gather` is called by neither yet. The item is the door, and it is additive: every
  existing test is the same world.
- ARCHITECTURE §4.9b gains the sentence: a venue's schedules come through the same door as a
  market's, and a venue module that builds another party's schedule is deciding for it.

behaviour changes; the tree is green with nothing else touched.
