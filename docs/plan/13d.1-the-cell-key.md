# Item 13d.1 — A cell's key carries its age, its tenure and its wealth

**Objective.** A household cell stands for one possible household with a multiplicity, and what
makes two households different is in its KEY (XI-15). Today the key is `region | cohort | bank`, and
three things 13d needs are not in it: how old the people are, whether they own the roof over them,
and how much they have. Everything 13d could not build ran aground on the same rock, and it is one
rock.

**Why it is one item and not three.** The blocker is identical in all three cases: a cell carries
its holdings PER MEMBER in whole pieces (Law 8), so anything that moves people between two cells has
to move value between two cells — and a leg's `qty` must equal `perMember × weight` on each side at
once, which two weights in the millions share no useful number for. `merge` cannot help: it refuses
two cells whose per-member state differs, which is exactly what handing an estate over makes true.
A weight event alone destroys units, because per-member state stays put while the total falls.

**The answer is that nothing should move.** If the thing that differs is IN THE KEY, then crossing a
boundary is a SPLIT — exact, per-member state and all — and the split cell simply carries a
different key. Nobody appears, nobody disappears, no value crosses, and the register never has to
find a quantity two weights agree on. That is what this item builds, and it is why it is the one
item that unblocks four of 13d's steps.

**Read first.** XI-15 (all, and `world/cells.ts` with it); Households A2, A2.b, F1, F1.a, F1.b, F2,
F3; Housing C1–C4, D1; Labour B3, E4; `registry/registry.ts`'s `CellKeyDimension`; 13b.1's key data.

**Clauses this item meets.** Households A2.b (wealth and tenure as ways households differ), F1, F1.a,
F1.b, F2, F2.a, F3; Housing C1 (a household mortgage), C4 (its foreclosure), D1; Labour E4; XI-15.

---

### Steps

- [ ] **The key gains three dimensions as DATA** (`CellKeyDimension`): `age` (a band, not a year —
      the band a person is in is a fact and the year inside it is not something this world tracks),
      `tenure` (owns the roof, rents it), `wealth` (a band). Nothing branches on any of them; they
      are dimensions, and what reads them is `keyOf` like everything else.
- [x] **A split by key**: `ctx.cells.reKey(cell, members, key, cause)` — the one new kernel event,
      and it is a SPLIT with a different key on the part that moved, which is the only way a weight
      moves between keys without value moving with it. It refuses a key the registry does not
      declare and a count that is not whole people.
- [x] **Ageing is a re-key** at the band boundary, at the rate the band's own span implies (a SHAPE
      with its death written: an age in the key is a band, so the ages inside it are spread evenly
      until something says otherwise).
- [x] **Retirement is the same event**, at the band the registry says is past working age. Labour's
      `participates` reads the band rather than the cohort.
- [ ] **Mortality is a re-key too**, and it is the interesting one: what a cell of dead people holds
      passes to the survivors of the SAME key, so the split is the dead, the survivors keep the
      state, and the dead cell holds its own share and is ceased to a named successor holding an
      identical per-member state — which `merge` already allows, because it is a renaming.
- [ ] **Tenure makes a household mortgage possible** (Housing C1): a cell in the `owns` band holds
      dwellings per member as whole ones, because the band is defined so that it does; a cell in the
      `rents` band holds none. Buying a roof is a re-key, and it is the same event ageing is.
- [ ] **Labour E4**: a cell that lost its earner misses its mortgage service, through item 5's event.
- [ ] **Wealth as a band** closes Households A2.b: the same income in different hands is different
      demand because the hands differ, and how much they have is one of the ways.
- [ ] Tests: the population is conserved across every re-key; no value crosses a key boundary; a
      band's occupancy moves and its per-member state does not; a cell that owns its roof pays no
      rent and a cell that rents pays somebody named.
- [ ] Coverage re-marked; record entry; delete this file; worklist row → done; **13d resumes**.

### Guard

The temptation is to let value cross a key boundary "just this once" — an estate to another cohort's
cell, a dwelling to a household that has just bought one. Every one of those is the defect this item
exists to remove, and the register will refuse it: a leg on a cell must be denominated per member,
and two cells of different weights have no such denomination. If a step here seems to need one, the
key is wrong, not the rule.
