# Project Phoenix — the implementation

> **This file is the ordered plan of what is left, rewritten on 2026-09-15 from a full adversarial
> review of every document and EVERY ENGINE SOURCE FILE** (`packages/engine/src`, 187 files, read
> line by line; every claim below names the file and line it was checked at; one rig world was
> assembled and stepped once to measure what the code does). It replaces the file of the same name.
> `docs/RECORD.md` carries the review's entry. The working findings file the review kept as it read
> (502 numbered findings) is rolled into Part 4 and every one of them is positioned in an item.
>
> **One item at a time, in the order in Part 1 (Law 10).** An item is done when its exit holds, its
> steps are ticked, `docs/RECORD.md` has its entry and `docs/COVERAGE.md` is re-marked — one commit
> (Law 14). When an item closes, delete its section; the record says what it closed.
>
> **Every step names a file, a change and a test.** A step that could be read two ways was
> rewritten until it could not. *Delete* names the read that replaces it (Law 19). *Measure* says
> what number, from what read, at what scale. A step marked **REMAKE** replaces a system's module
> rather than patching it: the review's advice is that four things are remade, not repaired — the
> cell representation (0b′), the phase assembly (0a), the bank's credit view (17.0′) and the
> opening (22a) — because each defect found in them is one design decision expressed in many places.
>
> **The rule on tests.** Lint and typecheck run whenever. The SUITE is a measurement and runs at the
> end of a module (Law 11). **"Does the world assemble and step" is the contract every module signed
> and is checked by `npm run check:opens` (item 0.9), which runs before lint in `npm run check` and
> may run at any time.** Eleven items closed on a world that threw at assembly.

### 0.3 What the world does when it opens

With the nine patches applied (thrown away after the run), the rig stepped 22 periods. Scale: 3
banks, 18 firms, 12 household cells, 8 funds, 146 markets, about 340 clearing sessions a period.

| 1 | 232 | 2 | 326 | 2 | 9 | 889 | 44 |

| 20 | 329 | 0 | 319 | 15 | 14 | 2,910 | 302 |
| 22 | 347 | 0 | 319 | 12 | 22 | 3,168 | — |

- **Nothing trades**, and the full read found why, in order of weight: (1) blocker 16 shuts every
  bank's lending; (2) blocker 17 stops every firm's investment; (3) `banks/index.ts:1738`
  `holderReservation` takes no obligor, so every desk's view of every bond is the same
  credit-blind number ± its own cost of funds (BK3, BK21, CB1, ST1, MM14): every bid is the same
  price and once inventory targets are met `skew` pushes every desk the same way; (4) a household
  offers labour only if it has an outlook on `benefit`, which exists only after a non-wage receipt
  (H1); (5) half of every cell's spare cash is bid every period for a dwelling nobody builds (H4);
  (6) every fund is wound up at `patience` periods because nobody subscribes enough to pay a
  manager's wage (FD10).
- **The population of parties grows 44 → 302 in 20 periods and never shrinks.** Cells split on
  every partial event (labour hire and separation: F7; ageing and death: H2; a household decision:
  H3) and `cells.merge` cannot fire because `sameState` requires identical lots in the same order
  (R2). This is the cell representation, not an omission (0.6, item 0b′).
- **The audit total rises monotonically** (30 → 174) because the units family reports every split
  child as "two cells standing for the same seat" (A1).

**The four remakes.** The review's advice, stated once here and carried by the items named:

1. **Cells (0b′, REMAKE of `parties/`, `register` cell sides, `ledger/settlement.ts`
   `validateCellSide`/`cellSide`/`shareFor`, `world/cells.ts`, the `flows` and `units` families).**
   XI-15 as implemented — holdings PER MEMBER in whole pieces, a partial event SPLITS, merge only
   on identical state — cannot express ageing into an existing cell, any cell↔cell trade (the
   `commonGrain` is the lcm of two arbitrary weights: M3), a cell paying a cell (W5), or a bounded
   cell count (H2, F7, H3), and it contradicts Part XII's one-cell-per-key (A1). The remake: a
   cell holds TOTALS in whole pieces with an integer weight; per-member is a READ (`total /
   weight`, a number, never a holding); a leg on a cell moves whole pieces of the total; a weight
   event that moves `n` members moves `floor(total × n / weight)` pieces and the remainder stays
   with the cell (a named holder, no residual); merge requires identical KEY only; a partial event
   moves pieces, never splits. The equity account stays per member as a read. Every law is kept;
   the count is bounded by the key space; cells trade with cells.
2. **Phase assembly (0a).** Anchors resolved after every module is collected, by a topological
   sort; cycles derived from anchors; a missing anchor named after collection.
3. **The bank's credit view (17.0′, REMAKE of `banks/quote.ts`, the `holderReservation` half of

   model per borrower from the bank's own record (annualised: BK8), an LGD from its own recoveries
   (BK7), a required yield per OBLIGOR (BK3), a capital charge per grade (RP14), a desk view that
   is the bank's own outlook of the name (BK21), a coupon struck from a per-name reservation (CB3).
   Every corporate market's two sides come from this.
4. **The opening (22a).** No stated print; opening holdings at cost; the first sessions print from
   posted reasons; FX has no rate until a pair trades.

## Appendix — the lessons this file exists to keep

1. **A `done` row and a `MET` mark are claims.** `check:opens` is the falsifier and runs before lint.
2. **"Do not measure mid-build" is not "never measure".**
3. **A missing sector is an item; a defect is evidence.**
4. **A finding leaves this file only by its item closing**, as a ticked step that names it.
5. **A performance step that changes a ratio on the ladder is not a performance step.**
6. **Insert at the dependency position and say where**, never append.
7. **A test that names a party is asserting a world that no longer exists.**
8. **A stated opening price is an imported equilibrium** however small the number.
9. **A docstring that describes a mechanism the code does not contain is a defect** (Law 16); the review found twelve.
10. **One design decision expressed in many places is remade, not patched**: cells, phases, the credit view, the opening.
